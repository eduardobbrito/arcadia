Arcadia for Dia

Spaces inside a profile, mini windows, cleaned links and Clear.

Set it up

  1. Pin Arcadia to the toolbar. Click its icon, or press , to open the spaces menu.
  2. Add a space. Choose “New space” in the menu. Dia's sidebar then shows the space you are in; the others are folded away as groups, or sit in their own windows.
  3. Move between spaces. Use the menu (keys 1 to 9 jump straight to a space), or give “next space” and “previous space” a shortcut below.
  4. Try a mini window. Shift-click any link, or press . Press inside it to move the page into your main window.
  5. Want separate logins? That is what Dia's own profiles are for. Arcadia's spaces share the logins of the profile they are in; add Arcadia to each profile where you want spaces.

Shortcuts

Dia keeps and its own command bar; Arcadia does not touch them. The browser assigns a shortcut only when the keys are free.

Privacy

Arcadia makes no network requests. Its pages are locked by a content-security policy that blocks every outside connection, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

It keeps no record of what you browse: no visited pages, no clicks, no history of its own. The only things saved are your spaces and these settings, in this profile.
